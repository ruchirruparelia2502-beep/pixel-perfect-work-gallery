Course Preamble & Prerequisites

This course imparts rigorous principles governing modern decentralized software architectures. Students analyze message passing models, logical clocks, distributed agreement paradigms, state-machine replication, and scalable partitioned datastores. Practical laboratory sessions emphasize high-concurrency systems programming, Raft consensus implementation, and containerized microservice fault injection.

Mandatory Prerequisites: CS202: Computer Networks (Min Grade B) CS204: Operating Systems (Min Grade B)
